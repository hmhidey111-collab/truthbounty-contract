## Namespace ownership

Every ERC-7201 / custom storage namespace declared under `contracts/` is owned by exactly one
defining contract and is recorded in `deployments/namespace-inventory.json`. The
inventory is generated by `node scripts/check-namespace-collisions.mjs --write-manifest` and is
verified in CI by the same script without the flag.

Rules enforced by the V2-SC-036 detector:

- A namespace identifier may be declared at most once across the entire codebase.
- Two declarations must not resolve to the same bytes32 slot constant.
- A derived contract must not redeclare a namespace already defined by an ancestor.
- A namespace identifier must not be renamed while its resolved slot stays the same.

Any upgrade that would repurpose an existing namespace must instead append a new ERC-7201
namespace with a fresh identifier and a fresh resolved slot. The detector fails CI otherwise.
